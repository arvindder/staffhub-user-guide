Frequently Asked Questions
==========================

General Questions
-----------------

How do I reset my password?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

1. Go to the login page
2. Click "Forgot Password"
3. Enter your email address
4. Check your email for reset instructions
5. Follow the link to create a new password

Can I access StaffHub on mobile devices?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Yes! StaffHub has a mobile-responsive design and can be accessed from any smartphone or tablet browser.

Scheduling Questions
--------------------

How far in advance can I create schedules?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

You can create schedules up to 6 months in advance. This helps with long-term planning.

Can I copy a previous schedule?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Yes! When creating a new schedule, you can select "Copy from existing schedule" and choose a previous schedule to duplicate.

User Management Questions
-------------------------

What happens when I delete a user?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Deleting a user will:
* Remove their access to the system
* Archive their historical data
* Remove them from future schedules

Their past schedule data will still be available in reports.

How do I change a user's role?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

1. Go to the Users section
2. Click on the user you want to modify
3. Click Edit
4. Change the Role field
5. Save the changes

Technical Questions
-------------------

What browsers are supported?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

StaffHub supports:
* Chrome (latest version)
* Firefox (latest version)
* Safari (latest version)
* Edge (latest version)

I'm experiencing slow performance. What should I do?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* Clear your browser cache
* Check your internet connection
* Try a different browser
* Contact support if the issue persists

Notifications
=============

StaffHub's notification system keeps you informed about important events and updates in real-time.

Notification Types
------------------

The platform sends notifications for:

* **Schedule Changes**: When your schedule is updated or published
* **Time-Off Requests**: Status updates on your time-off requests
* **Shift Reminders**: Reminders about upcoming shifts
* **System Alerts**: Important system-wide announcements
* **Messages**: Direct messages from managers or administrators

Managing Notifications
-----------------------

Viewing Notifications
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

1. Click the bell icon in the top navigation bar
2. View all your recent notifications
3. Click on a notification to view details

Notification Settings
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

To configure your notification preferences:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Notifications**
2. Choose your preferred notification methods:

   * **Email**: Receive notifications via email
   * **In-App**: Show notifications in the application
   * **Push**: Enable browser push notifications (if available)

3. Select which types of notifications you want to receive
4. Click **Save** to apply your preferences

Marking Notifications as Read
-----------------------------

* Click on a notification to mark it as read
* Click "Mark All as Read" to clear all unread notifications
* Notifications are automatically marked as read after 7 days

Email Notifications
-------------------

If email notifications are enabled, you'll receive emails for:

* Important schedule changes
* Time-off request approvals/denials
* System announcements
* Weekly schedule summaries (optional)

Reporting
=========

StaffHub provides comprehensive reporting capabilities to help you analyze your organization's data.

Available Reports
-----------------

The platform includes several pre-built reports:

* **Attendance Report**: Track staff attendance
* **Hours Worked**: Calculate total hours worked by staff
* **Schedule Compliance**: Monitor schedule adherence
* **Cost Analysis**: Analyze labor costs

Generating Reports
------------------

To generate a report:

1. Navigate to the **Reports** section
2. Select the type of report you want
3. Choose your date range and filters
4. Click **Generate Report**

Exporting Reports
-----------------

Reports can be exported in multiple formats:

* PDF
* Excel (XLSX)
* CSV

Click the **Export** button and select your preferred format.

Custom Reports
--------------

For advanced users, StaffHub supports custom report creation:

1. Go to **Reports** > **Custom Reports**
2. Click **New Custom Report**
3. Define your report parameters
4. Save and run your custom report

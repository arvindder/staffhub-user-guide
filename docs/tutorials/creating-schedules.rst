Tutorial: Creating Your First Schedule
=======================================

This tutorial will walk you through creating a schedule for your team.

Step 1: Access the Scheduling Section
-------------------------------------

1. Log in to StaffHub
2. Click on **Scheduling** in the main navigation menu

Step 2: Create a New Schedule
------------------------------

1. Click the **New Schedule** button
2. Enter a name for your schedule (e.g., "Week of January 15")
3. Select the start and end dates

Step 3: Add Staff Members
-------------------------

1. In the "Add Staff" section, select the staff members you want to include
2. You can select individuals or entire departments
3. Selected staff will appear in the schedule grid

Step 4: Assign Shifts
---------------------

1. Click on a cell in the schedule grid (day + staff member)
2. Select the shift type (Morning, Afternoon, Night, etc.)
3. Set the start and end times
4. Click **Save** to confirm

Step 5: Review and Publish
---------------------------

1. Review your schedule for any conflicts or issues
2. Click **Publish** to make the schedule visible to staff
3. Staff members will receive notifications about the new schedule

Tips
----

* Use shift templates to speed up schedule creation
* Check for conflicts before publishing
* Communicate schedule changes to your team

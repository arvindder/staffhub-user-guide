Tutorial: Setting Up Notifications
===================================

Learn how to configure notification settings to stay informed about important events in StaffHub.

Step 1: Access Notification Settings
-------------------------------------

1. Log in to StaffHub
2. Click on your profile icon in the top-right corner
3. Select **Settings** from the dropdown menu
4. Click on **Notifications** in the settings sidebar

Step 2: Choose Notification Methods
-----------------------------------

You can receive notifications through multiple channels:

Email Notifications
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

1. Toggle **Email Notifications** to ON
2. Verify your email address is correct
3. Choose which events should trigger email notifications:

   * Schedule changes
   * Time-off request updates
   * System announcements
   * Weekly summaries

In-App Notifications
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

1. Toggle **In-App Notifications** to ON
2. Notifications will appear in the bell icon in the top navigation
3. You can customize the sound and display preferences

Push Notifications (Optional)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

1. Toggle **Browser Push Notifications** to ON
2. Your browser will prompt you to allow notifications
3. Click **Allow** to enable push notifications

Step 3: Configure Notification Preferences
------------------------------------------

Set your preferences for different notification types:

* **Immediate**: Receive notifications as soon as they occur
* **Digest**: Receive a summary at specific times (e.g., daily at 9 AM)
* **Off**: Disable notifications for specific event types

Step 4: Test Your Settings
---------------------------

1. Ask a colleague to make a test schedule change
2. Verify you receive the notification through your preferred method
3. Adjust settings if needed

Best Practices
--------------

* Enable email notifications for critical events
* Use in-app notifications for day-to-day updates
* Set up digest mode for less urgent notifications
* Review your notification settings periodically
